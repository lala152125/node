Simple Daily Actions That Make a Real Difference

Ditch Single-Use Plastics

Carry reusable bags, bottles, coffee cups, straws, and containers. Refuse plastic cutlery and bags whenever possible.

Save Energy & Water

Turn off lights, unplug devices, take shorter showers, fix leaks — small habits add up fast.

Choose Sustainable Transport

Walk, bike, use public transit, or carpool. One less car trip per day = ~4 kg less CO₂.

Sort Waste & Recycle Properly

Separate recyclables, compost food scraps, avoid landfill when you can.

Eat More Plants, Less Meat

Try “Meatless Monday” or reduce portion sizes — livestock farming is a major source of emissions.

Plant Trees & Support Green Spaces

Join local tree-planting events, grow plants at home, or donate to reforestation projects.
